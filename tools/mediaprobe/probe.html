Camera Roll Probe

The personal cloud generates every thumbnail in the browser, so the VPS never touches a byte. That only works if this device can decode what the camera actually produces. Point this page at real photos and videos from the phone you intend to use.

Waiting for files Capabilities below are probed on load. The pipeline verdict needs at least one real photo.

Input

Or drag them here. Nothing is uploaded — every measurement below runs locally in this tab. Include a portrait shot, a video over 100 MB, and a ProRAW file if the phone shoots them.

Results

What a pass means. Native decode succeeded, the thumbnail encoded as WebP, and SHA-1 throughput is high enough that hashing a 500 MB video will not stall the tab.
What a warn means. The pipeline works, but on a slower tier — the heic2any WASM fallback (~1.2 MB, fetched once), a DNG preview extraction, or a PNG thumbnail where WebP encoding was unavailable.
What a fail means. This browser cannot build a thumbnail for that file, which in practice means HEVC video outside Safari. The upload still proceeds and the original is stored intact; the row goes to the Tier 3 ffmpeg worker on the VPS (spec §5.4), which extracts the poster frame by byte-range within a couple of minutes.
